        I admit, this may be a shitty portfolio website. It's not super sleek or minimalistic, and certainly doesn't put the emphasis on me as a software developer.
        But I can honestly say that IDGAF!
      - >-
        I've never been a fan of how most everyone in the (undergrad) tech industry has a website, often devoid of personality.
        Maybe that's the meta for getting jobs, but I think that's boring.
        That's why I had a basic, 60 line long website since 2023 - since I didn't like the whole idea of a personal portfolio website.
      - >-
        But after playing some ATLUS games (P3R, P5R, Metaphor), I realized that UI doesn't have to be boring and soulless, and I could incorporate my favourite thing (video games) with what I dreaded (personal websites)!
      - >-
        That's why even though this site may be shitty for the job search, I'm not planning on changing it anytime soon.
      - >-
        Go to the next pages to see my inspiration and fun facts about this website!
  - imageSrc: /assets/memorandum-images/metaphor-main-menu.webp

    body:
      - >-
        As you may have realized, this website is heavily inspired by video game menus, especially the main menu of Metaphor: Refantazio.
        I tried to keep the design and animations of the website fairly close to Metaphor's design.
      - >-
        if you've played Metaphor, see if you can spot which of my menu designs correspond with which in-game menu designs!
  - imageSrc: /assets/memorandum-images/metaphor-display-settings.webp
    imageAlt: 'Metaphor: Refantazio Display Settings Menu'
    body:
      - >-
        One especially difficult part of mimicking game menu design for this website was viewport responsiveness.
      - >-
        Games have the luxury of only designing their UI for certain aspect ratios, but websites do not!
      - >-
        Another limitation is the performance of web browsers on devices with different specs.
      - >-
        To work around this, I added an animation toggle so devices that chug too much when loading the various visual effects can turn them off!
