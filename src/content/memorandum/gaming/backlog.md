        I find that when it comes to gaming, I'm either hyperfixated on a game or constantly switching games.
        This has the unfortunate side effect of creating a backlog of games I'm halfway through, and so when I try to start playing again, I forget what's going on!
      - >-
        However, I recently saw a video that really resonated with me, and I'm trying to implement it in my gaming: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LlXaaa6FefE
      - >-
        I think one of the biggest strengths of video games compared to other forms of media is the control that you, the player, have on your experience, and this is something I feel is easy to lose sight of. Sometimes I try to be a completionist when I know that I won't enjoy it, or force myself to listen to dialogue I know I won't care about. By cutting out these moments, I think one can stay more immersed in games, thus hopefully remedying the cycle of constantly switching games.
